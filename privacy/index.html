Privacy Policy

Lloyd's Market Calendar connector — cal.opmodal.ai

This policy applies to the Lloyd's Market Calendar MCP connector served at cal.opmodal.ai and to the Lloyd's Deadlines calendar feed published at calendar.subscriptionmarket.news.

What this service is

The connector answers questions about the Lloyd's of London business timetable: public deadlines published by Lloyd's, with editorial commentary from opmodal. It is read-only. It does not create, modify or act on any data on your behalf.

Data we handle

  • No accounts. The connector requires no sign-in and holds no personal data.
  • No personal data. Tool results contain only public timetable information and opmodal's commentary. We do not collect names, email addresses or any identifiers from connector use.
  • Server logs. Standard access logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, endpoint) are kept for operational and security purposes for up to 30 days.
  • No cookies, no tracking. The connector endpoint sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Data sources and accuracy

Deadline data originates from the official Lloyd's business timetable. Always verify against the official timetable at lloyds.com before acting. opmodal's commentary is editorial opinion, not legal or compliance advice.

Third parties

The connector is delivered over Cloudflare's network (TLS termination and tunnel transport). Cloudflare processes request metadata as part of that transport. No connector data is sold or shared for advertising.

Subscribing to our briefings

If you choose to subscribe to the Submission Market briefing or the calendar feed, that is a separate opt-in handled by our newsletter platform (beehiiv) under its own privacy policy. The connector itself does not require or request any of it.
